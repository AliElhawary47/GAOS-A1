## AI Running Cost (for your own margin calculation)

GAOS uses DeepSeek as the primary AI provider. Actual costs for a typical deployment:

| Usage level | Monthly AI cost | Example |
|-------------|----------------|---------|
| Light (50–100 AI calls/day) | £0.50–£2 | 1–2 active modules |
| Medium (200–500 AI calls/day) | £2–£8 | Full Team, small business |
| Heavy (500–1,000 AI calls/day) | £8–£20 | Full Team, busy trades company |

Groq fallback is free-tier (no cost). Module 36 emails a monthly cost report automatically.

**At £999/mo Full Team subscription and £10/mo AI cost, gross margin is ~99%.**
